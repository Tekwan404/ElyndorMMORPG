# Ranked Arena 1v1 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Execute each task test-first, review its diff, then proceed. Do not enable the PvP entry point until the complete match and settlement contract is verified.

**Goal:** A server-authoritative, ranked 1v1 arena with Honor and leaderboard, without changing PvE outcomes.

**Architecture:** Use existing combat actor, ability, damage, effect, talent, event and reconnect primitives. Introduce explicit opposing-player ownership and a PvP-only terminal settlement path. PostgreSQL owns queues, match records, Honor, ratings and idempotent settlement; the in-memory combat runtime owns active ticking and is recoverable/cancellable after restart.

**Tech Stack:** .NET 10, EF Core/PostgreSQL, SignalR, Vue 3/TypeScript, Vitest, xUnit, Playwright.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-28-pvp-arena-1v1-design.md`

## Global constraints

- No open-world PvP, 2v2, Honor shop inventory, wagers, gear loss, raid or party changes.
- No monster impersonation, duplicate combat formulas, client-authoritative result, PvE loot/XP/death finalization, or ranked match without two real player actors.
- Keep PvE behavior unchanged; feature-gate entry until all supported class/build tests and CI pass.
